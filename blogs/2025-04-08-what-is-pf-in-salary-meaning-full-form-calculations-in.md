In salary terms, “PF” means Provident Fund—a retirement savings where both employee and employer contribute. In the realm of employment and remuneration, it is essential to understand the various components that make up an employee’s salary. This article aims to delve into the concept of PF in salary, explaining its types and PF eligibility.
