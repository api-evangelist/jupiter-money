Jupiter Money outlines the requirements for obtaining a credit card in India, noting that banks weigh income, employment status, age, and existing debt beyond just credit score. Eligibility typically requires ages 23-60, monthly income of Rs.15,000-25,000 for salaried applicants, and a CIBIL score of 750+.
